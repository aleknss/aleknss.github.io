description: "Ordenna is an automatic, accessible, and low-cost inventory management system, developed as a Final Degree Project. It uses an ESP32-CAM that takes periodic overhead photos of shelves where items are marked with colored stickers (🔴🟡🔵). A FastAPI (Python) application with OpenCV analyzes each image to detect, count, and classify products, storing results in a structured database. The information is displayed on a web interface with charts and user access for clients. It is designed to adapt from company warehouses to a home pantry."
